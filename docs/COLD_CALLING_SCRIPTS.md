# High-Conversion B2B Cold Calling & Outreach Scripts

## 1. The Gandhinagar Local Business Cold Call Script

### Scenario: Local store has Google Maps listing with positive reviews, but no website.
- **Sales Rep**: "Hello, is this the owner or manager of [Business Name]?"
- **Owner**: "Yes, speaking. What is this about?"
- **Sales Rep**: "Namaste sir/ma'am. I was looking for [category, e.g., car repairs / cafes / dental services] near [Area, e.g., Kudasan / Infocity / Sector 11] on Google. I noticed that while your competitors have their own website where customers view menus and book directly, your Google listing doesn't have an official website attached."
- **Owner**: "Yes, we don't have one right now."
- **Sales Rep**: "We help local businesses in Gandhinagar get an affordable, clean website set up in 48 hours to get direct customer orders without hefty platform commissions. Would it be okay if I sent a 1-minute WhatsApp preview demo of what your website could look like?"
- **Owner**: "Sure, send it over on this number."
- **Sales Rep**: "Wonderful, I'll send it across immediately from our official number. Have a great day!"

## 2. Category Pitch Angles

| Category | Primary Pain Point | High-Converting Pitch Angle |
|---|---|---|
| **Auto Garage / Car Care** | Missed emergency/maintenance bookings | "Direct appointment scheduling with instant WhatsApp confirmation" |
| **Cafe / Bakery** | High aggregator delivery commissions (25-30%) | "Direct digital QR menu & delivery orders with 0% commission" |
| **Dentist / Clinic** | Patient appointment queuing | "Instant online doctor consult booking & timing slot scheduler" |
| **Hardware / Boutique** | Product inquiry phone calls | "Interactive digital product catalog with instant inquiry buttons" |
| **Salon / Spa** | Chair idle time | "Online service package selector with automated reminders" |
